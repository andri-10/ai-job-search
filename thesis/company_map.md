# Master Thesis Company Map

**Purpose:** a versionable pipeline for potential March-August/September 2027 Master-thesis placements. This is a research and outreach list, not an application record. Keep actual submitted applications in the gitignored `job_search_tracker.csv` and `documents/applications/`.

**Candidate requirements:** paid, English-speaking, degree-integrated placement; individual and defensible 30-ECTS thesis; technical mentor and data/tool access; workable confidentiality/IP terms; verified host-country work authorisation.

**Status vocabulary:** `research` → `watching` → `outreach-ready` → `contacted` → `role-found` → `applied` → `closed`.

## How to use this map

1. Start with the `Priority 1` rows and check each official career page monthly from October 2026, then weekly from November 2026 through February 2027.
2. Look for `Master thesis`, `thesis`, `internship`, `working student`, `graduate research`, `AI`, `software`, `backend`, `data`, `optimisation`, and `systems engineering`.
3. Before outreach or applying, complete the feasibility checks in the row's notes: compensation, English as working language, thesis scope, academic supervision, confidentiality/IP, and immigration/work permission.
4. Move only an actual, chosen role into `job_search_tracker.csv`; this map remains the longlist and research record.

- [ ] Scope can produce an individual 22-26 week Master thesis from March 2027.
- [ ] Company mentor, data/tool access, and thesis agreement path are plausible.
- [ ] Confidentiality/IP terms can permit examination and defence.
- [ ] Internship/work permission has been checked for an Albanian citizen holding an Italian student residence permit.
- [ ] Next action and follow-up date are recorded above.
